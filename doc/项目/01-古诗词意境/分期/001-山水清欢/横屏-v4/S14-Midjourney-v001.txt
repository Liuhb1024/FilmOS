An immense windless Dongting Lake on an autumn moonlit night, a small low blue-green island resting near the low horizon. A modest softly luminous moon and a single faint veil of cloud above gently misted silver-blue water. The lake feels like an unpolished mirror, never a hard glass reflection. Inspired by Liu Yuxi’s affectionate silver-plate and green-shell metaphor, expressed as real lake and island.
